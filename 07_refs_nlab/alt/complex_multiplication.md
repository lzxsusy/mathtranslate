# 复乘  |  complex multiplication
concept: 椭圆曲线的自同态环严格大于 Z，即含虚二次序的作用。
level: B
citation_zh: [来源：MathWorld 词条「Complex Multiplication（复乘）」，\url{https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ComplexMultiplication.html}（2026-11-14 访问）。]
citation_en: MathWorld, "Complex Multiplication", https://mathworld.wolfram.com/ComplexMultiplication.html (accessed 2026-11-14).
verified: 2026-11-14 实抓该页（HTTP 200），正文原文："A complex Abelian variety of dimension n has complex multiplication iff its rational endomorphism algebra contains a commutative semisimple Q-subalgebra of dimension 2n (Milne 2020, Proposition 3.3). For a complex elliptic curve, this means that the endomorphism ring is larger than Z."。
note: 复乘椭圆曲线的 L 函数等于某个 Hecke 特征形式的 L 函数，是 Langlands 对应的原型例子。
