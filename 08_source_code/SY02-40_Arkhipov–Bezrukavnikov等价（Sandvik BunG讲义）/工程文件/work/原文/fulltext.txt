THE ARKHIPOV-BEZRUKAVNIKOV EQUIVALENCE
COLTON SANDVIK
Abstract. These expository notes accompany my BunG seminar talk on the
Arkhipov-Bezrukavnikov equivalence. First, we will setup the machinery of
central sheaves and Wakimoto sheaves. Then, we will explain the geometry
of coherent sheaves on the Springer resolution. We will use this to build a
functor from coherent sheaves on the Springer resolution to the antispherical
Hecke category. Finally, we will relate the antispherical Hecke category with
the Iwahori-Whittaker category.
