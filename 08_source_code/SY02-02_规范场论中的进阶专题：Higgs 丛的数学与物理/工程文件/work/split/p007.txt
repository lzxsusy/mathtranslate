2.3. Parabolic Higgs bundles.
The notion of Higgs bundles can be generalized
in several directions, and one which has caught the attention of mathematicians
and physicists during the last decades is the case of parabolic Higgs bundle, fol-
lowing the generalization of vector bundles to parabolic bundles [74].

compact Riemann surface Σ of genus g with marked points p1, . . . , pn satisfy-
