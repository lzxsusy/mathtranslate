geometric conjecture. However, measurements in ShvN that we are interested in can be

13.8. Sanity check for the Iwasawa-Tate case. One of the most important examples
of relative Langlands duality is the Iwasawa-Tate case, where G = Gm acts on X = A1 and
ˇG = Gm acts on ˇX = A1. The proof of Conjecture 13.21 for this case was given in Theorem

proof, but instead do a simple sanity check by comparing Hom spaces of both sides. In this
subsection, we work on the Betti setting exclusively. A more detailed account of this sanity
check can be found in Sect. 12.2.3 of BZSV.
