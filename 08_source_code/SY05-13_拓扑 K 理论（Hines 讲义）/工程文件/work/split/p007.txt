References for the following section are [AGP] chapters 9 and 12.
Deﬁnition 5. [AGP] 12.1.4 An additive reduced cohomology theory eE∗is a sequence of
contravariant functors { eEn}n∈Z from the category of pointed topological spaces to abelian groups
with the following properties:
• (suspension isomorphisms) eEn(ΣX) ∼= eEn−1(X), natural in X,
• (homotopy invariance) if f ≃g : (X, x0) →(Y, y0) then f∗= g∗: eE∗(X) →eE∗(Y ),
• (exactness) for each pair (X, A) there is an exact sequence
eEn(X ∪CA) →eEn(X) →eEn(A)
(maps induced by inclusions, CA the reduced cone on A),
• (additivity) eEn(∨αXα) ∼= Q
α eEn(Xα), induced by the inclusions.
Representability of cohomology theories are mediated by the following objects (proofs deﬁ-
nitely omitted).
Deﬁnition 6. An Ω-presepectrum is a sequence of pointed spaces {En}n∈Z and weak homo-
topy equivalences ϵn : En →ΩEn+1.
The hard direction of the following theorem follows from Brown’s representability theorem,
[AGP] theorem 12.2.22.
Theorem 2 ([AGP] 12.3.2, 12.3.3). An Ω-prespectrum determines an additive reduced cohmol-
ogy theory eE∗on the category of pointed topological spaces via
eEn = [X, En]∗.
Conversely, an additve reduced cohomology theory eE∗on the category of pointed CW-complexes
determines an Ω-prespectrum {En}n∈Z such that
eEn = [X, En]∗.
