# 好归约  |  good reduction
concept: 椭圆曲线可约化为剩余域上非奇异曲线的性质。
level: B
citation_zh: [来源：MathWorld 词条「Good Reduction（好归约）」，\url{https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GoodReduction.html}（2026-11-14 访问）。]
citation_en: MathWorld, "Good Reduction", https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GoodReduction.html (accessed 2026-11-14).
verified: 2026-11-14 实抓该页（HTTP 200），正文原文："the valuation ring. The special fiber of that model is then a smooth proper variety over the residue field"；"an elliptic curve with an integral Weierstrass equation and unit elliptic discriminant has good reduction. Its reduced equation remains nonsingular"。
note: 本讲即用整 Weierstrass 方程与判别式单位判定；好归约等价于 $p \nmid N_E$（导子指数为 0）。
