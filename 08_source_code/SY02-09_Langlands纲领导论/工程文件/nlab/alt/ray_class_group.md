concept: 模 m 下与主理想同余的真分式理想类群，记 Cl_m(K)。
level: A
citation_zh: [来源：J. W. S. Cassels and A. Fr\"ohlich (eds.), \textit{Algebraic Number Theory}, Proceedings of an instructional conference organized by the LMS, 第 VII 章（本讲参考文献）。]
citation_en: J. W. S. Cassels and A. Frohlich (eds.), Algebraic Number Theory, Proceedings of an instructional conference organized by the LMS, Chapter VII (bibliography of Lecture 2).
verified: 书目核对（未逐页翻原文）：lecture02.tex 的 references 把 CF 第 VII 章（整体类域论）列给类域论题材
