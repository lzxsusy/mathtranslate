\section{Twisted Künneth formula\label{sec:kunneth-formula}}
After introducing equivariant sheaves, it remains for us to establish the Künneth formula, which is what we essentially used in the proof of theorem \ref{thm:twisted-tensor-P1-bundle}. We start with establishing the dg enhancement of the triangulated category of quasi-coherent sheaves, which contains dg enhancements of bounded derived categories of coherent sheaves and perfect complexes as dg full subcategories.

We customly write $\Perf (X;\mathfrak{U})_{dg}$ for the full dg subcategory of perfect complexes (locally \emph{isomorphic} to bounded complexes of vector bundles).
\begin{prp}\label{prp:verifying-dg}
    $\Coh (X;\mathfrak{U})_{dg}$ is a dg category.
% ---- 原文行 3600 ----
\end{prp}
\begin{proof}
    It remains for us to check that the composition $\comp$ is associative. This can be checked directly and with a great care of signs.
\end{proof}
\begin{prp}\label{prp:dg-enhancement}
$\Coh(X,\mathfrak{U})_{\dg}$
is a dg-enhancement of $D^b(\Coh(X))$.
\end{prp}
\begin{proof}
    Recall that for flasque $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules $\mathcal{F}$ we have $\check{H}^p (\mathfrak{U},\mathcal{F})=0$ for $p>0$ (\cite[Chapter 3, Proposition 4.3]{Hartshorne77}) and any injective $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules are flasque (\cite[Chapter 3, Lemma 2.4]{Hartshorne77}). Since global sections on affine varieties are exact functors, we see that the \v{C}ech complex $\check{C}^{\bullet} (\mathfrak{U};-)\colon\Coh (\mathcal{O}_X)\to\Ch (\Bbbk)$, regarded as a functor between abelian categories, is exact.\par
    Given a complex of sheaves $\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}\in\Ch (\mathcal{O}_X)$, let $\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}\to\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet}$ be the double complex of its injective resolution, then from exactness we see that $\check{C}^{\bullet} (\mathfrak{U};\mathcal{F}^{\bullet})\to\check{C}^{\bullet} (\mathfrak{U};\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet})$ is a quasi-isomorphism of chain complexes. We can therefore replace $\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}$ by the total complex $\Tot^{\bullet} (\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet})$ of injective resolutions.\par
    The \v{C}ech complex $\check{C}^{\bullet}\left(\Tot^{\bullet} (\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet})\right)$ can then be written as a double complex. Using the aforementioned fact about injective $\mathcal{O}_X$ modules we see that $\displaystyle\check{H}^{\bullet} (\mathcal{I}^{p,q})$ is supported in degree $0$ for any $(p,q)$. Spectral sequences associated to double complexes \cite[\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/012X}{Section 012X}]{stacks-project} provide us a spectral sequence $E^{p,q}_d$ so that $E_2^{p,q}=\displaystyle\check{H}^q\left(\mathfrak{U};H^p(\Tot^{\bullet}(\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet}))\right)$, which is supported at $q=0$, and therefore the spectral sequence degenerates at the first page and therefore we have isomorphisms\[
    H^p\left(\Tot^{\bullet}(\check{C}^{\bullet} (\mathfrak{U};\Tot^{\bullet} (\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet})))\right)\cong H^p (\Tot^{\bullet} (\mathcal{I}^{\bullet,\bullet}))\cong H^p (\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}).
    \]
    Now for any objects $\mathcal{F}^{\bullet}$ and $\mathcal{G}^{\bullet}$, we have isomorphisms of $\field$-modules\[
    H^0\hom^{\bullet}_{\Coh (X)} (\mathcal{F}^{\bullet},\mathcal{G}^{\bullet})_{dg}\simeq\bigoplus_{p\in\mathbb{Z}}\hom_{\Coh (X)} (\mathcal{F}^p,\mathcal{G}^p).
    \]Together with the fact that shifting by $[1]$ in $H\Mod (\Coh(X)_{dg}))$ preserves $\Coh (X;\mathfrak{U})_{dg}$, we finish the proof.
\end{proof}
\subsection[equivariant dg categories]{$G$-equivariant derived dg-categories.} 
Let $G$ be an affine gorup scheme and let
% ---- 原文行 3620 ----
$\mathfrak U:=(U_i)_{i\in I}$
be an affine open cover of $X$
such that each $U_i$ is $G$-invariant.
For a $G$-equivariant $\mathcal O_X$-module $(\mathcal H,\Theta)$,
its \emph{$G$-invariant Čech cochain complex} with respect to
$\mathfrak U$ is
given by a collection of abelian groups
$$
\check C_G^p\big(\mathfrak U,(\mathcal H,\Theta)\big)
:=
\prod_{i_0<\cdots<i_p}
\mathcal H(U_{i_0\cdots i_p})^G
\quad\text{for }p\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge0},
$$
where
$U_{i_0\cdots i_p}:= U_{i_0}\cap\cdots\cap U_{i_p}$,
with a differential
$
\delta:
\check{C}_G^p\big(\mathfrak{U},(\mathcal{H},\Theta)\big)
% ---- 原文行 3640 ----
\rightarrow
\check{C}_G^{p+1}\big(\mathfrak{U},(\mathcal{H},\Theta)\big)
$
defined by
$$
(\delta s)_{i_0\cdots i_{p+1}}
:=
\sum_{j=0}^{p+1}(-1)^j
\left.
s_{i_0\cdots\widehat{i_j}\cdots i_{p+1}}
\right|_{U_{i_0\cdots i_{p+1}}}
\in
\mathcal{H}(U_{i_0\cdots i_{p+1}})^G
$$
for
$i_0<\cdots<i_{p+1}$,
where we denote by
$s=\left(s_{i_0\cdots i_p}\in\mathcal{H}(U_{i_0\cdots i_p})^G\right)_{i_0<\cdots<i_p}.$
