\section{Introduction}
The Mirror Symmetry phenomena was originally discovered by physicists and later formulated mathematically in various different forms. It was firstly understood as two Calabi-Yau manifolds having mirror symmetric Hodge diamonds, and then as relations between periodic integrals and the Gromov-Witten invariants. In 1994, Kontsevich \cite{Kontsevich95} proposed a framework to understand the mirror symmetry phenomena systematically as an equivalence between triangulated categories, one from coherent complexes of sheaves on an algebraic variety and another from Lagrangian submanifolds of a symplectic manifold. This was known as the \emph{Homological Mirror Symmetry} proposal. More precisely in modern language, given an expectedly mirror symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$ and an algebraic variety $(Y,J)$, we expect a quasi-equivalence of $A_{\infty}$-categories \[
    \mathcal{F} (X,\omega)\simeq\Coh (Y,J)
\]between a version of the \emph{Fukaya category} of the symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$ and the dg category of complexes of coherent sheaves on $Y$. Knowing Homological Mirror Symmetry, we are then expected to derive enumerative version of mirror symmetry via purely algebraic constructions \cite{KKP08}.\par
However, Homological Mirror Symmetry proposal didn't address the question of how the mirror pairs are constructed, and the corresponding geometric construction was proposed by Strominger-Yau-Zaslow \cite{SYZ96} in 1996. Roughly speaking, suppose a symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$ admits a fibration $X\to B$ whose fibres are Lagrangian tori, then we can construct a mirror space $\check{X}\to B$ by dualizing the fibre torus. There will be a natural complex structure $J$ on the dual space in good cases so we get a candidate mirror space $(\check{X},J)$. This construction was then generalized by the famous work of Gross and Siebert \cite{GS06,GS10,GS26} to the situation when $X\to B$ is a Lagrangian torus fibration with possibly elliptic singularities.\par 
In \cite{Fukaya05}, Fukaya proposed an approach to construct a mirror functor $\mathcal{F} (X,\omega)\to\Coh (\check{X},J)$ when $\check{X}$ is obtained from $X$ via SYZ-like construction. Roughly speaking, instead of naively taking dual Lagrangian fibrations, we look at the moduli space of Lagrangian tori (and their ``closures'') objects in the Fukaya category $\mathcal{F} (X,\omega)$, so that the existence of mirror functor is almost by definition. Significant partial progress under this proposal aligning the SYZ construction with Homological Mirror Symmetry was made, by Abouzaid \cite{Abouzaid21} when $X\to B$ is a smooth torus fibration and $\pi_2(B)=0$, and very recently Bai-Haney \cite{BH26} verified a situation when $X\to B$ fits into the Gross-Siebert program with some extra technical assumptions via intrinsic mirror symmetry.\par
In this paper, we look at the mirror pair $(X,\check{X})$ with $A$- and $B$-models swapped. In other words, we want to understand the following question:
\begin{ques}\label{Q:HMS-toric-degeneration}
    Suppose that $(X_t)_{t\in\mathbb{C}}$ is a toric degeneration family and $(Y,\omega_{p(t)})$ is the mirror symplectic manifold to $(X_t, J_t)$. Is there a quasi-equivalence of $A_{\infty}$-categories \[
% ---- 原文行 180 ----
        \Coh (X_t,J_t)\simeq\mathcal{F} (Y,\omega_{p(t)})?
    \]
\end{ques}
Note that answers to question \ref{Q:HMS-toric-degeneration} when $X_t\equiv X_{\Sigma}$ is a toric variety with moment polytope $\Sigma$ have been answered by Fang-Liu-Treumann-Zaslow \cite{FLTZ12} following an earlier idea of Bondal \cite{Bondal06} and an unpublished work of Bondal-Ruan, where the mirror $(Y,\omega)=\left((\mathbb{C}^{\ast})^n,\Lambda\right)$ is a Landau-Ginzburg model-like symplectic manifold, or more rigorously a \emph{Liouville sector} where the Fukaya category we talk about is the \emph{partially wrapped Fukaya category}, using the language of \cite{GPS20,GPS24M}. Sectorial Descent, established in \cite{GPS24S} and is expected to be mirror to proper descent of coherent complexes, should quickly imply Homological Mirror Symmetry results for the central fibre $\Coh (X_0,J_0)\simeq\mathcal{W} (Y_0,\omega_0)$ of the toric degeneration family. Seidel's proposal \cite{Seidel02} then suggests that $(X_t,J_t)$ should be mirror to a compactification $(Y,\omega_{p(t)})$ of $Y_0$, where $\omega_{p(t)}$ keeps track of the volume near the compatification data.\par
Before proceeding on this direction, there's a sanity problem to check. Given a toric degeneration family $(X_t,J_t)_{t\in\mathbb{C}}$, we can always replace the central fibre $X_0$ by its \emph{log blow-up} $\Bl_Z (X_0)$ along a divisor $Z$ with non-trivial log structure. This operation is local to the fibre at $0$ and should not change its smoothings, and therefore we wonder the following question:
\begin{ques}\label{Q:log-blow-up-mirror}
    What is the mirror space to log blow-ups? Does mirror to log blow-up affect the compactification?
\end{ques}
Answer to the second part of \ref{Q:log-blow-up-mirror} should be clear if we believe in question \ref{Q:HMS-toric-degeneration}: it should not affect the compactification. However, to answer the second part we have to first understand the first question, i.e. to construct the mirror space to log blow-ups. In the simplest situation, suppose that $X_0=Z_1\cup_D Z_2$ is a union of smooth algebraic varieties along a divisor $D$, the log blow-up $\Bl_Z X_0=Z_1\cup\mathbb{P}\left(N_{Z_1} D\oplus N_{Z_2} D\right)\cup Z_2$ is obtained by replacing $D$ by the projectivization of direct sums of normal bundles of $D$ to $Z_1$ and $Z_2$, respectively. It then suffices for us to understand the mirror to the projective bundle $\mathbb{P}\left(N_{Z_1} D\oplus N_{Z_2} D\right)$, and then apply sectorial and proper descent to both models. Therefore the question we need to address is 
\begin{ques}\label{Q:mirror-projective-bundle}
    Given a smooth projective algebraic variety $X$, what is the mirror space to the projectivized bundle $\mathbb{P} (\mathcal{O}_X\oplus\mathcal{L})$ for some line bundle $\mathcal{L}$ on $X$?
\end{ques}
This is in some sense a local question, and the name \emph{local mirror symmetry}, originated from the work of Chiang-Klem-Yau-Zaslow \cite{CKYZ99}, referred to the study of Gromov-Witten curve counts on total space of anti-canonical line bundles over Calabi-Yau surfaces. Observe that for any line bundles $\mathcal{L}_1,\mathcal{L}_2$ over a scheme $X$ we have $\mathbb{P}_X (\mathcal{L}_1\oplus\mathcal{L}_2)=\mathbb{P}_X (\mathcal{L}_1\otimes\mathcal{L}_2^{-1}\oplus\mathcal{O}_X)$, the spaces considered in \cite{CKYZ99} are also close to the cases we are interested in.\par
Before we go to the statement of the main result, let's gi ve intuitions on what the mirror to $\mathbb{P} (\mathcal{O}_X\oplus\mathcal{L})$ should look like. Suppose that $F\to P\to B$ is a general holomorphic Kähler bundle (so fibres and bases are all Kähler manifolds and they produce global Kähler forms on the total space) such that both the fibre $F$ and the base $B$ admits a Lagrangian torus fibration onto some smooth manifolds $\Sigma (F)$ and $\Sigma (B)$, which induces a global torus fibration on $P$. From the SYZ perspective, the mirror should be obtained by dualizing the Lagrangian torus firations, hence resulting in a fibre bundle \[
    \check{B}\to\check{P}\to\check{F}
\]with arrows reversed. Therefore, we expect the mirror $\mathcal{Y}$ to $\mathbb{P} (\mathcal{O}_X\oplus\mathcal{L})$ to be a symplectic fibre bundle over mirror to $\mathbb{P}^1$, which is known by \cite{FLTZ12} to be the Weinstein sector $(\mathbb{C}^{\ast}, z+1/z)$, with fibre $Y$ mirror to the base manifold $X$. Symplectic fibre bundles over $\mathbb{C}^{\ast}$ with given monodromy data $\phi$ was previously studied by Kartal \cite{Kartal21}, who provided a beautiful formula to obtain the Fukaya category $\mathcal{W} (\mathcal{Y})$ from the Fukaya category $\mathcal{W} (Y)$ and the induced auto-equivalence $\phi\colon\mathcal{W} (Y)\to\mathcal{W} (Y)$. We therefore expect that the ``mapping cylinder category'', with the stops equipped, is quasi-equivalent to the coherent complexes of sheaves over the projective bundle $\mathbb{P}\left(\mathcal{O}_X\oplus\mathcal{L}\right)$, once $\phi$ is mirror to tensoring by $\mathcal{L}$. This, however, is different from the mirror symmetry expectation mentioned in \cite{Kartal21}.
\subsection{Statement of the result.} The main result of this paper is a complete answer to question \ref{Q:mirror-projective-bundle} and several implications. Let $Y$ be a smooth quasi-projective variety over $\mathbb{C}$, and let $L(Y,\xi)^{\times}\to Y$ be a principal bundle over $Y$ with fiber the algebraic torus $\mathbb{G}_m$ and with characteristic class $\xi\in CH^2 (Y)$. Let $L(Y,\xi)\to Y$ and $P(Y,\xi)\to Y$ be the associated line and projective line bundle over $Y$. Let $X$ be a symplectic manifold mirror to $Y$ and $\phi\colon X\to X$ a symplectomorphism such that $\phi\colon\mathcal{W} (X)\to\mathcal{W} (X)$ is mirror to the functor $-\otimes L(Y,\xi)^{-1}$ obtained by tensoring with the line bundle $L(Y,\xi)^{-1}$. The mapping cylinder $M(X,\phi)\to\mathbb{C}^{\ast}$ of $\phi$ is a fibre bundle over $\mathbb{C}^{\ast}$, and we write $z$ and $1/z$ to be the composed function $M(X,\phi)\to\mathbb{C}^{\ast}\to\mathbb{C}$.
\begin{thm}\label{thm:HMS-projective-bundle}
    For HMS pairs $(X,Y)$ and mirror automorphisms $(\phi,\xi)$ with $X$ Weinstein sector and $Y$ smooth quasi-projective, there exist a canonical $A_{\infty}$-functor\[
        \Coh\left(P(Y,\xi)\right)_{dg}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\Perf\mathcal{W}\left(M(X,\phi),z+\dfrac{1}{z}\right)^{op}
% ---- 原文行 200 ----
    \]
    which induces equivalences of triangulated categories after taking homotopy categories.
\end{thm}
It's well expected that inclusions $Y\xrightarrow[i_0]{i_{\infty}} P(Y,\xi)$ of $Y$ into $P(Y,\xi)$ as the zero-section or section at infinity are mirror to the Orlov functors $\mathcal{W} (X)\xrightarrow[\cup_0]{\cup_{\infty}}\mathcal{W} (M(X,\phi),z+1/z)$ sending $X$ to the fibre near $0$ or $\infty$, respectively. With this expectation, we can quickly obtain that 
\begin{crl}\label{crl:mirror-symmetry-line-principal-bundles}
    Under the same convention as Theorem \ref{thm:HMS-projective-bundle}, there exist quasi-equivalences of $A_{\infty}$-categories \[
        \Coh (L(Y,\xi))_{dg}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\Perf\mathcal{W}\left(M(X,\phi),z\right)^{op}
    \]and\[
        \Coh (L(Y,\xi)^{\times})_{dg}\xrightarrow{\simeq}\Perf\mathcal{W} (M(X,\phi))^{op}
        \]between coherent complexes of $\mathbb{G}_m$-principal bundles and line bundles over $Y$ and perfect modules over wrapped Fukaya categories of mapping cylinders built on the pair $(X,\phi)$ possibly with a stop.
\end{crl}
We want to note that, although the theorem is pretty general, we don't know many examples where both the Homological Mirror Symmetry $\Coh (Y)_{dg}\simeq\Perf\mathcal{W} (X)^{op}$ and the mirrors between (graded) symplectomorphisms $\phi\in\Symp (X)$ and derived auto-equivalences $\check{\phi}\colon\Coh (Y)\to\Coh (Y)$ are known. A few known cases are for toric Fano manifolds \cite{FLTZ12,Abouzaid09,Hanlon19,GPS24M} and tensor by toric vector bundles and for log Calabi-Yau surfaces \cite{HK22,HK25} and tensor by any line bundles. Recently the HMS result in Hacking-Keating was further generalized by Varolgunes \cite{Varolgunes26} but it's not known whether we have similar statements about mirror auto-equivalence groups.\par
A very recent work of Mamaev \cite{Mamaev26} identified the category $\Coh (P(Y,\xi))_{dg}$ with Fukaya categories with dg coefficients $\Perf\mathcal{W} ((\mathbb{C}^{\ast},z+z^{-1});\Coh (Y)_{dg})^{op}$, so combining the two results suppposedly gives the quasi-equivalence \[
    \Perf\mathcal{W} (M(X,\phi);z+z^{-1})^{op}\simeq\Perf\mathcal{W} (\mathbb{C}^{\ast},z+z^{-1};\Perf\mathcal{W} (X)^{op})^{op}
\]which verifies in special situations that Fukaya categories of fibre bundles over $(\mathbb{C}^{\ast}, z+z^{-1})$ should correspond to perverse Schobers of Fukaya categories.
