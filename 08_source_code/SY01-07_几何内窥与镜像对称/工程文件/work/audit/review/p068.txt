case of a brane supported at a Z2 orbifold singularity r ∈ MH . Such a singularity is
associated with an SO3 local system whose structure group reduces to O2 . We recall
that O2 is embedded in SO3 as the subgroup

and that any SO3 local system whose structure group reduces to O2 has symmetry

                                               

As usual, we will consider a generic local systems of this type whose group of automor-
phisms is precisely this Z2 . In the present context, W|r , the restriction of W to r × C,
is a local system whose structure group reduces to O2 , so it has a decomposition
(5.22)                             W|r = U ⊕ det U,
where U is a rank 2 local system, with structure group O2 , and det U is its determinant.
The central generator of Z2 acts as −1 on U and as +1 on det U .
   As explained in Section 2.2, the category of branes supported at the orbifold singu-
larity r is generated by two irreducible objects B+ and B− . Each is associated with a
skyscraper sheaf supported at r. They differ by whether the non-trivial element of Z2
acts on this sheaf as multiplication by +1 or by −1.
   What happens when we act on B+ or B− by the Wilson operator Wp ? Since B+ and
B− both have skyscraper support at r, Wp acts on either of them by tensor product
with the three-dimensional vector space W|r×p , the fiber of W at r × p. However, we
should be more precise to keep track of the Z2 action. In view of eqn. (5.22), there is a
decomposition W|r×p = U |p ⊕ det U |p , where the non-trivial element of Z2 acts as −1
on the first summand and as +1 on the second summand. So we have
                       Wp · B+ = (B− ⊗ U |p ) ⊕ (B+ ⊗ det U |p )
(5.23)                 Wp · B− = (B+ ⊗ U |p ) ⊕ (B− ⊗ det U |p ) .

### CHINESE TRANSLATION

$\mathrm{W}\to\mathcal{M}_H\times C$ 到点 $m\times p\in\mathcal{M}_H\times C$ 的限制：

[begin equation]
(5.19)
W_p\cdot\mathcal{B}=\mathcal{B}\otimes\mathrm{W}|_{m\times p}.

[end equation]

此陈述在任一复结构下都成立，因此我们可以把 $\mathcal{B}$ 看作 $(B,B,B)$ 型本征膜。换言之，它是 $\mathcal{M}_H$ 在复结构 $I$、$J$ 或 $K$（或其任意组合）下的 B-膜，并且在任一复结构下都是本征膜。在几何 Langlands 纲领中，人们主要关心复结构 $J$；但零点膜同时在三个复结构下都是本征膜这一事实会简化计算，这一点将逐渐清楚。

## 轨形奇点处的 B-膜

我们希望把上述分析重复到支撑在 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ 轨形奇点 $r\in\mathcal{M}_H$ 的膜的情形。这样的奇点对应于一个结构群约化到 $O_2$ 的 $SO_3$ 局部系统。回忆 $O_2$ 作为子群嵌入 $SO_3$：

[begin equation]
(5.20)

[begin pmatrix]

* & * & 0\\
* & * & 0\\
0 & 0 & \pm 1

[end pmatrix]

[end equation]

并且任何结构群约化到 $O_2$ 的 $SO_3$ 局部系统都以 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ 为对称群，由 $O_2$ 的中心元素生成：

[begin equation]
(5.21)

[begin pmatrix]

-1 & 0 & 0\\
0 & -1 & 0\\
0 & 0 & 1

[end pmatrix]
.

[end equation]

如通常那样，我们考虑这种类型的一般局部系统，其自同构群恰为这个 $\mathbb{Z}_2$。在当前语境下，$\mathrm{W}|_r$（即 $\mathrm{W}$ 到 $r\times C$ 的限制）是一个结构群约化到 $O_2$ 的局部系统，因此有分解

[begin equation]
(5.22)
\mathrm{W}|_r=U\oplus\det U,

[end equation]

其中 $U$ 是一个秩 2 局部系统，结构群为 $O_2$，$\det U$ 是它的行列式。$\mathbb{Z}_2$ 的中心生成元在 $U$ 上作用为 $-1$，在 $\det U$ 上作用为 $+1$。

正如 §2.2 所解释的，支撑在轨形奇点 $r$ 的膜范畴由两个不可约对象 $\mathcal{B}_+$ 与 $\mathcal{B}_-$ 生成。它们各自与支撑在 $r$ 的点层相联系；二者的区别在于 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ 的非平凡元素在该层上作用为乘以 $+1$ 还是乘以 $-1$。

当我们用 Wilson 算子 $W_p$ 作用于 $\mathcal{B}_+$ 或 $\mathcal{B}_-$ 时会发生什么？既然 $\mathcal{B}_+$ 与 $\mathcal{B}_-$ 都以点层支撑于 $r$，$W_p$ 对它们的作用便是与三维向量空间 $\mathrm{W}|_{r\times p}$（$\mathrm{W}$ 在 $r\times p$ 处的纤维）作张量积。不过，为追踪 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ 作用，我们应更精确些。由式 (5.22)，有分解 $\mathrm{W}|_{r\times p}=U|_p\oplus\det U|_p$，其中 $\mathbb{Z}_2$ 的非平凡元素在第一直和项上作用为 $-1$、在第二直和项上作用为 $+1$。于是

[begin equation]
(5.23)

[begin aligned]

W_p\cdot\mathcal{B}_+ &= (\mathcal{B}_-\otimes U|_p)\oplus(\mathcal{B}_+\otimes\det U|_p)\\
W_p\cdot\mathcal{B}_- &= (\mathcal{B}_+\otimes U|_p)\oplus(\mathcal{B}_-\otimes\det U|_p).

[end aligned]

[end equation]
