   The fibers of the map from M0H to CP1 are copies of C. For example, the fiber for
z = 0 is given by b1 = 1, b2 + ib3 = 0, and is parametrized by b2 . However, M0H is
not a holomorphic line bundle over CP1 , because the map from M0H to CP1 has no
holomorphic section. Rather, the structure group of the bundle M0H → CP1 is the
group of affine linear motions of C, that is the group of transformations of the form
x → ax + b, a ∈ C× , b ∈ C. We might describe M0H as an affine bundle of rank 1.
   The group of affine linear motions has a homomorphism to C× , by forgetting b. So
to an affine bundle of rank 1, there is always a canonically associated fiber bundle
whose structure group is C× . In the case of the quadric, the associated variety is the
cotangent bundle of CP1 . The quadric itself admits a holomorphic symplectic form:18

从 $\mathcal{M}_H^0$ 到 $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$ 的映射的纤维是一份份 $\mathbb{C}$。例如 $z=0$ 的纤维由 $b_1=1$、$b_2+\mathrm{i}b_3=0$ 给出，并由 $b_2$ 参数化。然而 $\mathcal{M}_H^0$ 不是 $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$ 上的全纯线丛，因为映射 $\mathcal{M}_H^0\to\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$ 没有全纯截面。相反，丛 $\mathcal{M}_H^0\to\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$ 的结构群是 $\mathbb{C}$ 的仿射线性运动群，即形如 $x\to ax+b$（$a\in\mathbb{C}^\times$，$b\in\mathbb{C}$）的变换群。我们可以把 $\mathcal{M}_H^0$ 描述为秩 $1$ 的仿射丛。

仿射线性运动群有到 $\mathbb{C}^\times$ 的同态（忘掉 $b$）。因此，对秩 $1$ 仿射丛，总有一个典范相伴的、结构群为 $\mathbb{C}^\times$ 的纤维丛。在二次曲面的情形，相伴的簇是 $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$ 的余切丛。二次曲面本身带有全纯辛形式：（脚注：乘性常数 $\sigma_0$ 是通过在由 $b_1,b_2,b_3$ 为实数所刻画的二循环上积分 $\Omega$，并与 [GW] 式 (3.77) 中 $\Omega$ 的上同调类的结果比较而确定的。}
