     An SL2 spectral curve D is defined by an equation
(5.10)                                 z 2 + det ϕ = 0,
where det ϕ is a quadratic differential on C. For C of genus g, a quadratic differential
has 4g − 4 zeroes. D is smooth if and only if the zeroes of det ϕ are distinct, in which
case the genus of D is 4g − 3.
   What happens when the structure group reduces to O2∗ ? The Lie algebra of O2∗ is
simply the abelian algebra of traceless diagonal matrices. So if (E, ϕ) is an SL2 Higgs
bundle whose structure group reduces to O2∗ , then ϕ locally takes the form

where ω is a holomorphic differential on C. This leads to det ϕ = −ω 2 , as a result of
which any zeroes of det ϕ are double zeroes. Near a double zero at, say, x = 0, with
x a local parameter on C, the equation for D looks something like z 2 − x2 = 0; the
point z = x = 0 is a double point. Each double point reduces by 1 the genus of the
normalization of D. For an O2∗ Higgs bundle, the zeroes are all double zeroes, so there
are 2g − 2 double points and the normalization of D is a curve D ′ of genus 2g − 1.
     The fact that the zeroes of det ϕ are double zeroes does not imply that globally
det ϕ = −ω 2 for a holomorphic section ω of the canonical bundle K. Rather, it implies
that det ϕ = −ω 2 where ω is a holomorphic section of K ⊗ V, for some line bundle
V → C of order 2. The case of interest to us is that V is non-trivial. (The case that V
is trivial is related to Higgs bundles whose structure group reduces to GL1 rather than
O2∗ .) Associated with the choice of V is an unramified double cover π ′ : D ′ → C. This is
a curve of genus 2g − 1, and is the normalization of D. Let τ : D′ → D ′ be the covering
map that commutes with π ′ . Then C = D ′ /{1, τ }. The element ω ∈ H 0 (C, K ⊗ V)
vanishes at 2g − 2 points p1 , . . . , p2g−2 . Above each such point pi there are two points
p′i , p′′i in D ′ , exchanged by τ , but only a single point pbi ∈ D. D is obtained from D ′ by
gluing together the pairs of points p′i and p′′i .
     What we have just described is a natural analog of the result of Section 5.1 for
a genus 1 curve C with one point of ramification. A curve of genus 1 has precisely
three non-trivial unramified double covers; these are the normalizations D′ of the three
singular spectral curves D.

生成的 $O_2$ 的二重覆盖。

$SL_2$ 谱曲线 $D$ 由方程

[begin equation]
(5.10)
z^2 + \det\varphi = 0

[end equation]

定义，其中 $\det\varphi$ 是 $C$ 上的二次微分。对亏格 $g$ 的 $C$，二次微分有 $4g-4$ 个零点。$D$ 光滑当且仅当 $\det\varphi$ 的零点互不相同；此时 $D$ 的亏格为 $4g-3$。

当结构群约化到 $O_2^*$ 时会发生什么？$O_2^*$ 的 Lie 代数就是无迹对角矩阵构成的阿贝尔代数。因此，若 $(E,\varphi)$ 是结构群约化到 $O_2^*$ 的 $SL_2$ Higgs 丛，则 $\varphi$ 局部地形如

[begin equation]
(5.11)
\varphi = 
[begin pmatrix]

\omega & 0\\
0 & -\omega

[end pmatrix]
,

[end equation]

其中 $\omega$ 是 $C$ 上的全纯微分。这导致 $\det\varphi = -\omega^2$，因此 $\det\varphi$ 的零点都是二重零点。在例如 $x=0$ 处的二重零点附近（$x$ 是 $C$ 上的局部参数），$D$ 的方程看起来像 $z^2-x^2=0$；点 $z=x=0$ 是一个二重点。每个二重点使 $D$ 的正规化的亏格减少 1。对 $O_2^*$ Higgs 丛，零点全是二重零点，故有 $2g-2$ 个二重点，而 $D$ 的正规化是亏格 $2g-1$ 的曲线 $D'$。

$\det\varphi$ 的零点都是二重零点这一事实，并不意味着整体上 $\det\varphi = -\omega^2$，其中 $\omega$ 是典范丛 $K$ 的全纯截面。它实际意味着 $\det\varphi = -\omega^2$，其中 $\omega$ 是 $K\otimes\mathcal{V}$ 的全纯截面，$\mathcal{V}\to C$ 是某个 2 阶线丛。我们关心的是 $\mathcal{V}$ 非平凡的情形。（$\mathcal{V}$ 平凡的情形与结构群约化到 $GL_1$ 而非 $O_2^*$ 的 Higgs 丛相关。）与 $\mathcal{V}$ 的选择相联系的是非分歧二重覆盖 $\pi': D'\to C$。这是亏格 $2g-1$ 的曲线，也是 $D$ 的正规化。设 $\tau: D'\to D'$ 是与 $\pi'$ 交换的覆盖映射。则 $C = D'/\{1,\tau\}$。元素 $\omega\in H^0(C,K\otimes\mathcal{V})$ 在 $2g-2$ 个点 $p_1,\dots,p_{2g-2}$ 处为零。在每个这样的点 $p_i$ 之上有 $D'$ 中的两个点 $p'_i,p''_i$，它们被 $\tau$ 交换，但 $D$ 中只有一个点 $\hat{p}_i$。$D$ 由 $D'$ 把成对的点 $p'_i$ 与 $p''_i$ 粘合而得。

刚才所描述的是 5.1 节结果对带一个分歧点的亏格 1 曲线 $C$ 的自然类比。亏格 1 的曲线恰有三个非平凡非分歧二重覆盖；它们就是三条奇异谱曲线 $D$ 的正规化 $D'$。

## Prym 簇

$SL_2$ 的 Hitchin 纤维 $\boldsymbol{F}$ 的一个开稠子集由满足 $\det\pi_*(\mathcal{L}) = \mathcal{O}$ 的线丛 $\mathcal{L}\to D$ 构成。完整的 Hitchin 纤维除了这些线丛之外，还参数化某些无挠层。
