6.6. Other Examples. In this section we look at other examples of categories of
fractional Hecke eigensheaves. First, we consider the abelian case, when G = C× or an
arbitrary torus. Then we consider the case of LG-local systems (where G is an arbitrary
reductive group), whose group of automorphisms is the center of LG. Finally, we discuss
the geometric Eisenstein series.

6.6.1. The Case Of C× . Let G = C× , so that LG = C× as well. Let E be a C× -local
system on C, which we will view as a rank one local system. The objects of the category
AutE are the data (F, α), where F is a D-module on Pic, the Picard scheme of C, and
                                               ∼
(6.12)                           α : H1 (F) −→ E ⊠ F.
Here H1 is the Hecke functor corresponding to the identity character C× → C× ,
                                      H1 (F) = p∗ (F),
where
                        p : C × Pic → Pic,         (x, L) 7→ L(−x).
This Hecke property automatically implies the Hecke property for the Hecke functors
Hn , n ∈ Z, corresponding to other characters of C× . The D-module Fourier–Mukai
equivalence, due to [La4, Ro], implies that F is a direct sum of copies of a particular
flat line bundle on Pic. From now on we will denote by F this flat line bundle.
   Since Pic is the disjoint union of its components Picn , n ∈ Z, corresponding to line
bundles of degree n, each D-module F on Pic decomposes into a direct sum
                                            M
(6.13)                                 F=      Fn .
                                             n∈Z

  The automorphism group of any rank one local system E is C× . Hence we have an
action of C× on the category AutE . It is given by the formula
                           λ · (F, α) 7→ (F, λα),        λ ∈ C× .
   Now let us describe the corresponding category of fractional Hecke eigensheaves.
Let C be the category whose objects are the direct sums of the D-modules Fn , n ∈ Z,
appearing in the decomposition (6.13) of the Hecke eigensheaf F. These are flat vector
bundles on the components Picn ⊂ Pic. The action of the category Rep(C× ) on C is
given by the formula
                                 [m] ⋆ Fn = Fn+m ,
where [m] denotes the one-dimensional representation corresponding to the character
C× → C× , a 7→ am .
  An object of the category Aut′E of fractional Hecke eigensheaves is then given by the
data (K, α), where K ∈ C and
                                           ∼
(6.14)                        α : H1 (K) −→ E ⊠ ([1] ⋆ K).
The isomorphism α gives rise to a unique isomorphism
                                      ∼
                      αn : Hn (K) −→ E ⊗n ⊠ ([n] ⋆ K),         n ∈ Z,
satisfying the required properties.

### CHINESE TRANSLATION

## 其他例子

本节我们考察分数 Hecke 本征层范畴的其他例子。首先考虑阿贝尔情形，即 $G = \mathbb{C}^\times$ 或任意环面。然后考虑 ${}^LG$-局部系统的情形（其中 $G$ 是任意约化群），其自同构群为 ${}^LG$ 的中心。最后我们讨论几何 Eisenstein 级数。

## \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{C
^\times$}{C×} 的情形}

设 $G = \mathbb{C}^\times$，于是 ${}^LG = \mathbb{C}^\times$。设 $\mathcal{E}$ 是 $C$ 上的 $\mathbb{C}^\times$-局部系统，我们把它视为秩一局部系统。范畴 $Aut_{\mathcal{E}}$ 的对象是数据 $(\mathcal{F},\alpha)$，其中 $\mathcal{F}$ 是 $\mathrm{Pic}$（$C$ 的 Picard 概形）上的 D-模，且

[begin equation]
(6.12)
\alpha : H_1(\mathcal{F})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal{E}\boxtimes\mathcal{F}.

[end equation]

这里 $H_1$ 是对应于恒等特征 $\mathbb{C}^\times\to\mathbb{C}^\times$ 的 Hecke 函子，

$$

H_1(\mathcal{F}) = p^{*}(\mathcal{F}),

$$

其中

$$

p : C\times\mathrm{Pic}\to\mathrm{Pic},\qquad (x,\mathcal{L})\mapsto\mathcal{L}(-x).

$$

这一 Hecke 性质自动蕴含对应于 $\mathbb{C}^\times$ 的其他特征的 Hecke 函子 $H_n$（$n\in\mathbb{Z}$）的 Hecke 性质。由 [La4, Ro] 给出的 D-模 Fourier--Mukai 等价蕴含 $\mathcal{F}$ 是 $\mathrm{Pic}$ 上某个特定平坦线丛的若干个副本的直和。今后我们用 $\mathcal{F}$ 记这个平坦线丛。

由于 $\mathrm{Pic}$ 是其各分支 $\mathrm{Pic}_n$（$n\in\mathbb{Z}$，对应于次数 $n$ 的线丛）的不交并，$\mathrm{Pic}$ 上的每个 D-模 $\mathcal{F}$ 都分解为直和

[begin equation]
(6.13)
\mathcal{F} = \bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{F}_n.

[end equation]

任何秩一局部系统 $\mathcal{E}$ 的自同构群都是 $\mathbb{C}^\times$。因此我们有 $\mathbb{C}^\times$ 在范畴 $Aut_{\mathcal{E}}$ 上的作用。它由公式

$$

\lambda\cdot(\mathcal{F},\alpha)\mapsto(\mathcal{F},\lambda\alpha),\qquad \lambda\in\mathbb{C}^\times

$$

给出。

现在描述相应的分数 Hecke 本征层范畴。设 $\mathcal{C}$ 是这样的范畴：其对象是 Hecke 本征层 $\mathcal{F}$ 的分解 (6.13) 中出现的 D-模 $\mathcal{F}_n$（$n\in\mathbb{Z}$）的直和。这些是分支 $\mathrm{Pic}_n\subset\mathrm{Pic}$ 上的平坦向量丛。范畴 $\mathrm{Rep}(\mathbb{C}^\times)$ 在 $\mathcal{C}$ 上的作用由公式

$$

[m]\star\mathcal{F}_n = \mathcal{F}_{n+m}

$$

给出，其中 $[m]$ 表示对应于特征 $\mathbb{C}^\times\to\mathbb{C}^\times$（$a\mapsto a^m$）的一维表示。

分数 Hecke 本征层范畴 $Aut'_{\mathcal{E}}$ 的对象于是由数据 $(\mathcal{K},\alpha)$ 给出，其中 $\mathcal{K}\in\mathcal{C}$ 且

[begin equation]
(6.14)
\alpha : H_1(\mathcal{K})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal{E}\boxtimes([1]\star\mathcal{K}).

[end equation]

同构 $\alpha$ 给出唯一的同构

$$

\alpha_n : H_n(\mathcal{K})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal{E}^{\otimes n}\boxtimes([n]\star\mathcal{K}),\qquad n\in\mathbb{Z},

$$

满足所需性质。
