   To construct MH (C; SO3 , θ) (or rather a Zariski open set corresponding to stable
bundles E), we must consider all Higgs fields ϕ ∈ H 0 (C, K ⊗ O(p) ⊗ W ), impose a
suitable condition on the polar part of Tr ϕ2 , and divide by Q. Before trying to do this,
let us discuss what will happen if we do not divide by Q.
   From the point of view of complex algebraic geometry, instead of considering SL2
Higgs bundles (E, ϕ) where E is a rank 2 holomorphic bundle with det E trivial, it is
natural to pick a fixed line bundle L and consider Higgs bundles (E, ϕ) with det E = L.
If one replaces L by L ⊗ N 2 , one can compensate for this by E → E ⊗ N . Modulo
this operation, all that really matters about L is its degree modulo 2. Apart from the
familiar case of L = O, we can consider a second component with (say) det E = O(p).
We write MH (C; SL∗2 ) for the moduli space of stable Higgs bundles of this type. We
sometimes refer to this as the improper component of the SL2 moduli space (and we
refer to the moduli space of ordinary SL2 Higgs bundles (E, ϕ) with det E trivial as
the proper component). If (E, ϕ) is a Higgs bundle with det E = O(p), then, upon
setting W = ad(E), we get an SO3 Higgs bundle (W, ϕ) with w2 (W ) 6= 0. All (W, ϕ)
can arise this way, and (E, ϕ) is determined uniquely from (W, ϕ) up to twisting E
by a line bundle of order 2. So MH (C; SL∗2 ) can be related to SO3 Higgs bundles by
analogy with (3.20):

(3.30)                    MH (C; SO3 , θ) = MH (C; SL∗2 )/Q.

   From the point of view of differential geometry, MH (C; SL∗2 ) can be constructed by
solving Hitchin’s equations with gauge group the compact group SO3 on a bundle with
w2 6= 0 and dividing only by those gauge transformations that can be lifted to SU (2).

Remark 3.4. The reasoning of Remark 3.3 may also be applied to the group SL2 to show
that MH (C; SL2 ) and MH (C; SL∗2 ) are isomorphic as complex symplectic manifolds
in complex structure I.

给出这个空间的直接描述将是有用的。$\mathcal{M}_H(C;SO_3,\theta)$ 参数化分歧 Higgs 丛 $(W,\varphi)$，其中 $W$ 是 $w_2\ne0$ 的 $SO_3$-丛。对我们的目的而言，只需在该模空间中构造一个 Zariski 开集，它对应于 $W$ 稳定的那些 Higgs 丛。据注 3.2，这意味着 $W=\mathcal{S}_1\oplus\mathcal{S}_2\oplus\mathcal{S}_3$ 是三个非平凡 2 阶线丛的直和。可以取 $\mathcal{S}_i=\mathcal{O}(p)\otimes\mathcal{O}(q_i)^{-1}$，其中 $q_i$ 是由 $(x,y)=(e_i,0)$ 给出的 2 阶点。作为 $SO_3$ 丛，$W$ 应具有非退化二次型，我们把它记作迹（即把 $W$ 视为伴随丛）。其定义如下：若 $s=s_1\oplus s_2\oplus s_3$ 是 $W=\mathcal{S}_1\oplus\mathcal{S}_2\oplus\mathcal{S}_3$ 的截面，则二次型为 $\Tr s^2=\sum_i s_i^2/(x-e_i)$。其想法是：$s_i^2$ 是 $\mathcal{O}(p)^2\otimes\mathcal{O}(q_i)^{-2}$ 的截面，而除以 $x-e_i$ 即可将其平凡化。

丛 $W$ 具有自同构群 $Q=\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$。事实上，作为平坦丛，$W$ 具有式 (3.26) 与 (3.28) 给出的单值 $V_1$ 与 $V_2^{(2)}$，而 $Q$ 是由对角元为 $\pm1$ 的对角矩阵组成的 $SO_3$ 子群。

为构造 $\mathcal{M}_H(C;SO_3,\theta)$（更确切地说，对应于稳定丛 $E$ 的 Zariski 开集），我们必须考虑所有 Higgs 场 $\varphi\in H^0(C,K\otimes\mathcal{O}(p)\otimes W)$，对 $\Tr\varphi^2$ 的极点部分施加适当条件，再除以 $Q$。在着手这样做之前，先讨论不除以 $Q$ 时会发生什么。

从复代数几何的观点看，与其考虑 $E$ 为行列式平凡的秩二全纯丛的 $SL_2$ Higgs 丛 $(E,\varphi)$，不如取定线丛 $\mathcal{L}$ 并考虑 $\det E=\mathcal{L}$ 的 Higgs 丛 $(E,\varphi)$。若把 $\mathcal{L}$ 换成 $\mathcal{L}\otimes\mathcal{N}^2$，可通过 $E\to E\otimes\mathcal{N}$ 补偿。模去这一操作，$\mathcal{L}$ 真正重要的只是其次数模 $2$。除熟悉的 $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{O}$ 情形外，我们还可以考虑第二个分支，比如取 $\det E=\mathcal{O}(p)$。我们把这类稳定 Higgs 丛的模空间记作 $\mathcal{M}_H(C;SL_2^*)$。有时称之为 $SL_2$ 模空间的 improper 分支（improper component）（而把 $\det E$ 平凡的通常 $SL_2$ Higgs 丛 $(E,\varphi)$ 的模空间称为 proper 分支）。若 $(E,\varphi)$ 是 $\det E=\mathcal{O}(p)$ 的 Higgs 丛，则令 $W=\mathrm{ad}(E)$ 便得到 $w_2(W)\ne0$ 的 $SO_3$ Higgs 丛 $(W,\varphi)$。所有 $(W,\varphi)$ 都可由此得到，而 $(E,\varphi)$ 在相差把 $E$ 扭转一个 2 阶线丛的意义下由 $(W,\varphi)$ 唯一确定。因此，通过与 (3.20) 类比，可把 $\mathcal{M}_H(C;SL_2^*)$ 与 $SO_3$ Higgs 丛联系起来：

[begin equation]
(3.30)
\mathcal{M}_H(C;SO_3,\theta)=\mathcal{M}_H(C;SL_2^*)/Q.

[end equation]

从微分几何的观点看，$\mathcal{M}_H(C;SL_2^*)$ 可通过在 $w_2\ne0$ 的丛上以紧群 $SO_3$ 为规范群求解 Hitchin 方程、并只对可提升到 $SU(2)$ 的规范变换取商来构造。
