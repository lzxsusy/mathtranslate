These ideas which grew out of the SYZ conjecture focus on the base of the
SYZ ﬁbration; even though we do not know an SYZ ﬁbration exists, we have a
good guess as to what these bases look like. In particular, they should be aﬃne
manifolds, i.e., real manifolds with an atlas whose transition maps are aﬃne linear
transformations. In general, these manifolds have a singular locus, a subset not
carrying such an aﬃne structure. It is not diﬃcult to write down examples of such
manifolds which we expect to correspond, say, to hypersurfaces in toric varieties.
