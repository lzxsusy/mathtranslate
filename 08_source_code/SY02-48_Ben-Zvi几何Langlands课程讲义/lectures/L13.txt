Next time, we’ll give another take on class ﬁeld theory, relating it to a categoriﬁed version of spectral
decomposition. We want to understand the relationship between local systems on Pic and sheaves on Loc to
be some sort of Fourier transform. We will spectrally decompose all local systems on Pic (not just character
local systems — like in the ordinary Fourier transform, we can try to write an arbitrary local system as a
combination of character local systems). On the B-side, looking at QC(Loc1(Σ)), our “basis” is points of
Loc1(Σ).
But our notion of “spectrum” is categoriﬁed. In the beginning of the class, we discussed how the spectrum
is some sort of adjoint or inverse to the functor assigning to a geometric thing (e.g. spaces) its ring of functions.
