Given a 3-manifold Y with a knot K ⊂Y , we can label K with an element V of K0(RepG∨). This

Electric-magnetic duality should identify the vector spaces in the A- and B-sides; on one side we have relatively
complicated operators, and on this side we have the simplest possible kinds of functions on character varieties
(spaces of connections). These two quite diﬀerent operators are labeled by the same set, K0(RepG∨), and
electric-magnetic duality should switch them. In general, this story is mysterious; we don’t understand these
vector spaces on the harder A-side well without assuming the conjecture, which is cheating.

Pick a representation V : G∨→GLn, so we get a map from functions on GLn/GLn to functions on G∨/G∨.
This is what we do when we talk about characters. Everyone has a favorite way of measuring matrices, which
is the characteristic polynomial; for G∨, there’s generally not a nice way to do this without picking V . Given
a matrix F, the characteristic polynomial is det(1 −tF). A fancy way to say this is to consider Λ∗Cn; take
the graded trace of F on this representation, and this is the characteristic polynomial. Now replace Cn with
V , and we can do this for elements of G∨. Number theorists sometimes conventionally consider 1/ det(1 −tF)
instead of the characteristic polynomial, though they contain the same information. This variant is the graded
trace of F acting on Sym∗V . This is a version of the geometric series formula 1/(1 −t) = 1 + t + t2 + . . . ,
and is also a version of Koszul duality, which exchanges exterior and symmetric algebras.
