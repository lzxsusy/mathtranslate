Abstract
This expository paper is an introductory text on topological K-theory and the Atiyah-Singer
index theorem, suitable for graduate students or advanced undegraduates already possessing a
background in algebraic topology. The bulk of the material presented here is distilled from
Atiyah’s classic “K-Theory” text, as well as his series of seminal papers “The Index of Elliptic
Operators” with Singer. Additional topics include equivariant K-theory, the G-index theorem,
and Bott’s paper “The Index Theorem for Homogeneous Diﬀerential Operators”. It also includes
an appendix with a proof of Bott periodicity, as well as sketches of proofs for both the standard
and equivariant versions of the K-theory Thom isomorphism theorem, in terms of the index
for families of elliptic operators. A second appendix derives the Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral
sequence. This text originated as notes from a series of lectures given by the author as an
undergraduate at Princeton. In its current form, the author has used it for graduate courses at
the University of Oregon.
